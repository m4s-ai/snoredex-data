# 30th Celebration — retained research, 2026-09-10

Eleven localized Snorlax renders are retained as SPEC-0507–SPEC-0517. Local product pages announce 2026-09-16; these are announced releases, not already-released inventory.

Every source and its scope is recorded in `sources.json`. Exact card observations and image hashes are in `observations.json`; `intake.json` uses durable repository images. `retained-pages.json` indexes the saved raw responses. Browser-only observations are explicitly recorded as reviewed fields, not represented as downloaded page bytes.

[Release overview](release-overview.html) is a compact, screenshot-friendly local page with all eleven original card renders and two explicitly expected placeholders for Korean and Simplified Chinese. Open it from a checkout or serve the repository locally. It uses the retained specimen images directly, contains no generated card artwork, and is a research presentation rather than a canonical identity or finish assertion. The page is not part of the public Pages artifact.

## Boundaries

- English, German, French, Italian, European Spanish, Latin American Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese, Traditional Chinese, Thai and Indonesian card faces were inspected separately.
- Canada and UK/Ireland announcements add regional release evidence, not new physical print identities.
- CN announces the set; the specific Simplified Chinese Snorlax identity remains unestablished. Korea is now positively announced in official news 21033 and its Korean packaging poster; the previously blocked routes became accessible. A Korean card checklist or exact anniversary Snorlax image has not been found. Neither missing checklist establishes absence.
- Official booster pages describe every card as foil (JP: キラカード6枚入り; TW/HK: 含6張閃卡; TH: การ์ดฟอยล์ 6 ใบ; ID: Berisi 6 lembar kartu hologram). This source-native product statement is retained; a render does not resolve the physical foil pattern or close the finish inventory.
- Tournament legality on September 25 is separate from the September 16 release. Japanese starter products have a separate October 16 date.
- The Indonesian fur-box news and product page show September 16 and September 25 respectively; do not transfer either box date to the booster.
