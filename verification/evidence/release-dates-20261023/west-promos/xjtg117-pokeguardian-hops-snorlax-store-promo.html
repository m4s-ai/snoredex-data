Hop's Snorlax Store Promo Revealed

Published on 28 February 2025 at 12:27

Hop's Snorlax from Journey Together will be a store promo for at least Singapore, Malaysia, Philippines. 

 

This promo includes a stamp of the Journey Together logo.
  • (1 / 1)
  • 1

Update 14-03-2025: Also confirmed for Gamestop USA / Canada and EB Games for Australia with the Gamestop logo.

Pick up $15 worth of Pokémon TCG products at GameStop to bring home a Hop’s Snorlax promo card. Australian players who purchase $15 worth of Pokémon TCG products at EB Games will receive a Hop’s Snorlax promo card.

And Canadians can buy $15 worth of Pokémon TCG products at GameStop to receive a Hop’s Snorlax promo card. Purchases can be made in-store or online.

According to the official Pokemon TCG Asia English page, you can obtain this promo at card shops from March 28 with a minimum purchase of either SGD25, RM80, or ₱1030 of Pokémon TCG products.

 

Please make sure to contact your local participating store for more information.

 

If a European store promotion is coming, or other countries we do not know. There is no information regarding this for now. Promotions happened in Europe before with Koraidon & Miraidon Store Promos with set logo stamp exclusive to Europe. Likely TPCi will publish an official info with more retailers participating in Journey Together store promos soon for other regions. Make sure to follow our social media as to not miss anything.
