## Outcome and next useful lead

All four finish questions remain unresolved by this round. A marketplace finish field, rarity
symbol, flat scan, card render, seller title, or lack of visible glare cannot establish physical
finish. XY-P 149's missing finish-specific page or incomplete search results also do not establish
absence; the earlier absence-based contradiction must not be repeated.

The useful next lead for each target is an owner-held card photographed or videoed at an oblique
angle under moving light, or a seller gallery that actually includes such a view. For XY-P only,
Mercari search results also surfaced the exact-card listings
<https://jp.mercari.com/item/m10188112030> and
<https://jp.mercari.com/item/m18040490655>; only their search-result titles/thumbnails were
visible in this round, so they remain uninspected leads and cannot support a finish claim. No
comparable angle-bearing public lead was found for XY2, 20th, or XY10. Any accepted future photo
must retain its original bytes with the listing/image URL, retrieval date, and SHA-256, and be
visually inspected before recording a finish.

The Mercari CDN image URLs rendered in the browser, but a normal local `urllib` request was refused
by the network environment. No access control was bypassed. Because none of the inspected images
provided positive finish evidence, no downloaded or retained photo is part of this evidence bundle.
