establish card identity where stated, but this round found no source that positively establishes a
physical finish. Marketplace labels, silent grading labels, absent glare and image alt text were not
treated as finish evidence. No specimen records or canonical data were changed.

## G2 — Rocket's Snorlax

Searches on 2026-09-29 included `"チームロケットのカビゴン" G2 ホロ なし`, `"Rocket's Snorlax"
G2 PSA non holo`, `"Rocket's Snorlax" "Non-Holo" "Gym 2" Japanese card photo angle`, and exact
PSA/CGC finish-label variants. Opened and inspected:

- [eBay listing 407142711848](https://www.ebay.com/itm/407142711848), titled “Rocket's Snorlax No.143
  Non-Holo 1997 Japanese Gym Challenge LP.” The front and back identify the Japanese Rocket's
  Snorlax card, but the front is a flat view. The seller title says “Non-Holo”; its condition text
  also mentions faint scratches on “holo,” so the listing metadata conflicts and neither phrase
  settles finish.
- [eBay listing 287537070730](https://www.ebay.com/itm/287537070730), titled “Rocket's Snorlax Team
  Rocket Holo Japanese 1st Ed 143 90 HP.” The front photos show broad sleeve glare without a
  distinctive foil pattern. Observed front image URLs included
  `https://i.ebayimg.com/images/g/cpAAAeSwTclqh360/s-l1600.webp` and
  `https://i.ebayimg.com/images/g/ARMAAeSwa1Bqh37C/s-l1600.webp`.
- [PSA certificate 94317877](https://www.psacard.com/cert/94317877/psa), the [PSA price guide](https://www.psacard.com/priceguide/non-sports-tcg-card-values/1999-poke-mon-japanese-gym-2/3921), and [PSA set composition](https://www.psacard.com/psasetregistry/tcg/company-sets/1999-pokemon-japanese-gym-2/composition/18081). These identify the card but do not name a holo or non-holo variety for it. The absence of a suffix is silence, not a non-holo claim.

Other opened catalog pages provide product identity or catalog finish fields only: [eBay product
page](https://www.ebay.com/p/26073649433), [Hareruya2](https://www.hareruya2.com/products/9013792604480),
[CardTrader](https://www.cardtrader.com/en/cards/rocket-s-snorlax-no-143-gym-booster-2-challenge-from-the-darkness),
and [Price-Base](https://shop.price-base.com/c/pokemon/xpansionchallengefromthedarkness/teamrssnorlax-r).
Exact PSA/CGC label searches did not surface a label that positively names this card's finish. A
useful next lead is a cert or collection photo that explicitly names the finish, or an un-sleeved,
angled front photo where the card surface itself can be assessed.

## DP1 — Snorlax Lv.35

Searches on 2026-09-29 included `"カビゴン Lv.35" DP1 ホロ PSA`, `"Snorlax Lv.35" DP1 non holo
Japanese`, `"Snorlax Lv.35" Japanese holo DPBP 174`, and exact PSA/CGC label variants. Opened and
inspected:

- [eBay listing 388623328567](https://www.ebay.com/itm/388623328567). The visible card face is the
  Japanese Snorlax Lv.35 card; the seller title uses DPBP#174. The observed front image is
  `https://i.ebayimg.com/images/g/Qt0AAOSw1stoWWDM/s-l1600.webp`; it is a flat face photo. The second
  photo is the card back, despite eBay's machine alt text calling it a “rainbow foil background.”
  DPBP#174 is not the collector number.
- [Official Pokémon Card search entry](https://www.pokemon-card.com/card-search/details.php/card/1082/regu/DP)
  identifies the card but exposes no finish field.
- [PSA Snorlax Master Set composition](https://www.psacard.com/psasetregistry/tcg/famous-figures/pokemon-snorlax-master-set/composition/21636)
  lists the DP1 Snorlax without a finish suffix. This does not establish non-holo.
- [TCG Republic DP1 list](https://tcgrepublic.com/category/subcategory_page_3477.html) labels the
  entry with DPBP#174 and a rarity symbol, but its finish wording is catalog metadata. The Shopee
  Malaysia listing [PTCG Pokemon Card Ver.2006 Snorlax Lv.35 DPBP-174](https://shopee.com.my/PTCG-POKEMON-CARD-VER.2006-Snorlax-LV.35-%E5%8D%A1%E6%AF%94%E5%85%BD-LV.35-DPBP-174-NON-HOLO-Japanese-GEN%27-90--i.92316348.27219363952)
  redirected to a login-required page; no login was attempted and no card photo was inspected there.

Opened [Hareruya2](https://www.hareruya2.com/products/9014396649792) and
[Suruga-ya](https://www.suruga-ya.jp/kaitori/kaitori_detail/G6824969) product pages and a Shopee
Philippines search result. These are catalog or seller metadata, not physical finish evidence. The
useful next lead is an angled, un-sleeved physical photo or a grading/collection label that explicitly
names the exact finish.

## EC5 — Snorlax 062/088

Searches on 2026-09-29 included `"Snorlax 062/088" Japanese non holo holo Mysterious Mountains`,
`"Snorlax 062/088" CGC Japanese Mysterious Mountains`, and the exact PSA label query
`"Snorlax" "Mysterious Mountains" "PSA" "062/088"`. Opened and inspected:

- [eBay listing 407054747223](https://www.ebay.com/itm/407054747223), an exact Japanese 062/088
  listing with 18 gallery images. The observed main front image is
  `https://i.ebayimg.com/images/g/CSgAAeSwwdtqTLCx/s-l1600.webp`; the card is shown flat. The other
  front views are likewise flat/cropped; remaining photos show the back or corners. Machine alt text
  describes several views as rainbow/holographic, but visual inspection did not show a physical foil
  surface. Item specifics say “Finish Regular,” “Common,” Japanese and 062/088; these are seller
  metadata, not a surface observation.
- [eBay listing 198669085269](https://www.ebay.com/itm/198669085269) has five images: a flat front,
  the card back and corner views. Its front establishes the exact Japanese card but supplies no
  angled surface evidence.

  JAPANESE MYSTERIOUS MOUNTAINS #062 SNORLAX MYSTERIOUS MOUNTAINS,” with subject Snorlax and card
  number 062. It does not name a holo/non-holo variety.
- [PSA Snorlax Master Set composition](https://www.psacard.com/psasetregistry/tcg/famous-figures/pokemon-snorlax-master-set/composition/21636)

Opened catalog references were [Hareruya2](https://www.hareruya2.com/products/9014551806272),
[TCG Republic](https://tcgrepublic.com/product/product_page_2000214550.html),
[DeckTradr](https://www.decktradr.com/cards/pokemon-japanese/576134-snorlax-062-088), and
[Cardonaut](https://cardonaut.com/ja/pokemon/cartes/PKM_ES5_JP_62-88_v0). Their “regular/standard/normal”
fields were not treated as physical evidence. The next useful lead is an un-sleeved angled photo with
reflection crossing the card face or an exact grading label that positively names the finish.
