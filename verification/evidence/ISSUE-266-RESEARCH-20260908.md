## Accepted observation

Bulbapedia revision 4587656 lists Snorlax 58/83 in Generations and explicitly includes Spanish
among the languages with reverse-holo cards. The matching Spanish identity already rests on
SPEC-0131. The scoped override therefore confirms only GEN 58 Spanish reverse-holo. It does not
promote the ordinary finish, introduce a stamp or edition, or close the finish list. The existing
set-era projector supplies the type-symbol pattern from its separate pattern source.

The [retained observation](issue-266-generations-reverse-20260908.json) records the exact revision,
card row, positive sentence, acquisition-response hash and applicability basis. The full response
is transport cache; the small observation is retained evidence. The source remains Bulbapedia,
not the identity-only photograph and not an owner decision.

physical printing: JU 27, KSS 26, XYPR 179 and SWSH 032. This is a physical-printing count only,
not a claim that every other release is free of every field-level gap.

  yielded an inspected Spanish slab/photo, so no printing or edition was accepted.
- **KSS 26:** the retained identity scan does not establish finish. The Russian owner finish
  decision applies to its recorded scope; it is not copied to Spanish.
- **XY179:** the [Spanish retailer box page](https://www.game.es/caja-pokemon-gx-snorlax-merchandising-134879)
  identifies the product but does not establish the required finish in its retrieved text.
  An Italian publisher foil description is not a Spanish manufacturing claim.
- **SWSH032:** a [Spanish pin-collection listing](https://www.mercadolibre.cl/pokemon-tcg-snorlax-pin-collection--espanol/up/MLCU109578057)
  names a foil promo but does not provide a retained, inspected exact card/number in this pass.
  It remains a product lead; the existing database scan remains identity-only.
- **SVP ES 184:** current catalogue rows already have holo printings, images, Promo rarity,
  month-precision dates and `mapped-by-explicit-equivalence`. The old issue list's blanket
  missing-image/finish description is stale. Do not relabel that mapping as directly `mapped`
  merely to satisfy an old progress counter.
- **JU 11:** the current physical printing has an unknown date while compatibility research
  rows carry an older date. Do not transfer that date automatically or count the whole release
  gap-free from the presence of a holo alone.

The issue stays open. Further closure requires exact positive finish/field evidence, not a
