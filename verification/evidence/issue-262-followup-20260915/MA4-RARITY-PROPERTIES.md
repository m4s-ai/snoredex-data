# Thai MA4: rarity versus physical properties

Research date: 2026-09-15. Manual web search and page reading only. No new canonical confirmation, specimen admission or set-wide finish rule.

## Findings

The official Thai MA4 announcement specifies 123 base designs plus special cards, five randomly assorted cards per booster and the 2026-03-20 release. It does not provide a rarity-to-finish specification:
https://asia.pokemon-card.com/th/archives/7073/

The Thai set checklist distinguishes C, U, R, RR, AR, SR, SAR and MUR. Its entries identify rarity, not a physical finish:
https://thaipkmn.shop/en/catalogue/?pct_set_id=1328

| Rarity | MA4-specific information found | Evidential limit |
|---|---|---|
| C / U | Checklist explicitly labels Common and Uncommon. Snorlax 091 is C. | No applicable statement found establishing all C/U as NonHolo. This unsuccessful search is not evidence that foil variants do not exist. |
| R | Thai retailer explicitly groups MA4 cards as R (Foil); another seller identifies 123/123 as R-Foil. | Positive seller descriptions, not an inspected specimen or exhaustive print specification. Exact foil pattern remains unresolved. |
| RR | Thai Mega Emboar ex 028/123 listing explicitly states Double Rare and Holo. | Applies to the offered card; not proof of every RR's exact surface or texture. |
| AR | Thai Probopass 139/123 and Raticate 146/123 offers explicitly state Full Art Holo. | Seller evidence for examples; no set-wide claim that every AR is untextured. |
| SR | Thai checklist includes 148–168/123 as SR. | No sufficiently clear Thai MA4-specific texture specification found. Do not import Japanese properties automatically. |
| SAR | Thai checklist includes 169–179/123 as SAR. | Special Art classification established at checklist level; physical texture not independently established in this pass. |
| MUR | Thai 180/123 listing explicitly describes Gold; checklist places 180–181/123 in MUR. | Gold description for 180 only; exact material, foil pattern and embossing not verified. |

## Consequence for issue #262

The set research strengthens R/RR/AR foil leads but does not supply the missing positive evidence for Snorlax MA4 091/123 NonHolo. Keep C rarity and physical finish separate. Do not assert that MA4 lacks reverse, mirror, Poké Ball or Master Ball variants merely because no qualifying result was found.
