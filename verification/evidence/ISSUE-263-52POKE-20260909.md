# Issue #263 — s5a F evidence and 52Poke image search

The owner supplied the missing 52Poke route and exact card image on 2026-09-09. SPEC-0489 retains the supplied PNG unchanged. The printed s5a F, 093/070 UR, Traditional Chinese card text and illustrator establish the local identity of U0602. The linked card article (revision 2150661) explicitly supplies the 2021-04-02 release date. Source-native UR is retained; no normalized rarity or physical finish is inferred.
