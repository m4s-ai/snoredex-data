Western sets — Spanish and Portuguese

The product articles list localized names for French, German and Italian but stop there. A missing entry in a name table is not proof of non-release, so these stay open rather than contradicted — and the XY179 promo showed why: no database carried its German, Italian or Spanish printings, yet physical copies of all three exist.

Japanese promos — cleared

All promo claims are now resolved. The decisive source was a collector database that lists one entry per market printing: it carries S-P Snorlax promos in six languages, so finding S-P 156 only in Japanese is evidence rather than a gap. Note that a Japanese retail campaign alone proves nothing — XY-P 149 came from a Marumiya promotion in Japan and the very same card from a Kisstick sausages promotion in Korea.

Japanese decks and collection products

Most of this block closed once the infobox release field was checked — deck products list their per-language release dates there rather than in a language table. What remains are products whose release field names only one market. Main expansions are a separate case and are all resolved: the Asian markets never received translations of them, so the printing turns up under a catch-up set instead. For the rest, a sealed product listing from a seller in that market is often the only evidence that exists.
