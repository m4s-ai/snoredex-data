What this is

This view shows every active confirmed physical printing in collector_catalogue.json, with its current field values and sources. It combines verified legacy printings with positively documented source-first releases. It is not a complete all-locality catalogue. The historical Cardmarket baseline remains provenance, not the table's membership boundary.

{{stats}}
Scope limits — read these before relying on anything here.
  • Physical cards only. Code cards are excluded. Language and finish evidence are separate; a language confirmation never establishes a finish.
  • Pending means unresolved, never absent. Missing source entries are gaps. Only an explicit collection-owner adjudication settles absence; otherwise contradictions remain disputed.
  • ES is European Spanish; ES-LA is Latin American Spanish. Localized source-first releases retain their own set codes and numbers. LATAM (es-419) is a separate physical edition in the locality graph, never implied by European Spanish (es-ES) or a Cardmarket filter.
  • Evidence must reach the exact card. {{needs_evidence}} claims remain needs-evidence in the data exports; unresolved candidates are excluded from this table.
